-- API root set for the pickles package: the in-circuit kimchi verifier.
--
-- The capstones are the package's reason to exist; everything the gadgets, the token
-- machine and the certificates prove must stay reachable from them, from the linearization
-- reflection, or from a check script (the `script-surface` blocks).

-- The capstones: a step circuit's must-verify slots are verified by it and the next wrap
-- circuit's finalize block, and a wrap circuit's step proof by it and the next step circuit's
-- finalize. Everything the package proves stays reachable from these.
Pickles.stepWrap_kimchiVerify
Pickles.wrapStep_kimchiVerify

-- The handover along a chain: the accumulator one link emits is an old accumulator of the
-- proof the next link verifies, step or wrap, unless Poseidon collides.
Pickles.WrapStepRun.mem_olds_or_collision
Pickles.StepWrapRun.mem_olds_or_collision

-- The capstones' typed public-input ties are the wire's public input: the step circuit's
-- statement values flatten to what `wrapPublicInput` commits to, and the wrap circuit's packed
-- statement, flattened (`stepPublicInput`), is the leaves' input followed by the zero
-- optional-feature cells.
Pickles.StepStatement.ofWrap_toFields
Pickles.stepPublicInput_eq_append
