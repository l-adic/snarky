-- API root set for the pickles package: the in-circuit kimchi verifier.
--
-- The two capstones are the package's reason to exist; everything the gadgets, the token
-- machine and the certificates prove must stay reachable from them, from the linearization
-- reflection, or from a check script (the `script-surface` blocks).

-- The capstones: a proof of each kind is verified by its two circuits, a step circuit's
-- must-verify slots are verified by it and the next wrap circuit's finalize block, and a wrap
-- circuit's step proof by it and the next step circuit's finalize. Everything the package
-- proves stays reachable from these.
Pickles.stepProof_kimchiVerify_vesta
Pickles.wrapProof_kimchiVerify_pallas
Pickles.stepWrap_kimchiVerify

-- The bridge from `stepWrap_kimchiVerify`'s public-input tie to the public input
-- `kimchiVerify` reads: the wrap public input is the packed statement, reduced.
Pickles.wrapPublicInput_toList
Pickles.wrapStep_kimchiVerify

-- The wrap circuit's packed statement is the wire's public input: the anchor for the
-- capstone's typed public-input tie.
