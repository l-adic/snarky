-- API root set for the pickles package (the in-circuit kimchi verifier, so far its
-- linearization slice). Consumed by the workspace dead-code pass
-- (formal/scripts/deadcode.sh); the axiom audit is pickles/scripts/check_axioms.lean.

-- The roots ARE the axiom gate's roots: the two linearization circuit theorems, one per
-- side of the cycle, the two reflection endpoints they rest on, and the two ft_eval0
-- circuit theorems built on them. Everything else the package proves or defines — the
